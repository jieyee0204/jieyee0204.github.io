Hi! I am Jie Yee Lim, a Ph.D. student in Quantitative Psychology at the Georgia Institute of Technology, advised by Dr. Audrey Leroux.

I received my master’s in Statistics, Measurement, Assessment, and Research Technology from the University of Pennsylvania and my bachelor’s in Psychology from Chung Yuan Christian University in Taiwan.

My research focuses on psychometrics and quantitative methods, including item response theory (IRT), multilevel modeling, and simulation studies. I am broadly interested in developing and evaluating statistical methods that improve the quality of psychological measurement.

Prior to my doctoral studies, I also worked on psycholinguistic research investigating code-switching among Malaysian multilinguals living in Malaysia and Taiwan.
