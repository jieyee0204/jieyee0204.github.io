Travel Log

Outside of research, I travel as much as I can. I have visited twenty-nine countries and love collecting the small details of each place — the food, the languages, and the way people move through their cities.

Originally from Malaysia, I have also lived in Taiwan, Germany, and the United States. I speak Mandarin, English, and Malay, and have studied German and Japanese — though these days little beyond the greetings has stuck.
